A few things to know.

Do I need an AI key?

Built-in repairs for common issues work without one. Connect your own AI provider in Settings for unfamiliar errors and chat. Your provider may charge for usage.

Will it change things without asking?

Repairs ask for approval by default. You can enable Autopilot in Settings for supported repairs. Pause fixes stops repairs while monitoring continues.

Does it keep running in the background?

Yes. Closing the main window keeps monitoring active in the system tray. The Windows installer sets it to start when you sign in. Use Quit in the tray menu to stop it.

How do I install it?

Download SystemAutoBot-windows.zip from a GitHub release, extract it, and open Install.cmd. If no installer is listed, a public build has not been published yet. Setup instructions are also in the README.

What stays on my PC?

Your fix history stays on your PC. When you use AI, your conversation and check summaries are sent to the provider you connect.
