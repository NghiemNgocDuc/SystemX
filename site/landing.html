How it works

One path for every problem.

  1. Watch

    Windows events live, plus cheap checks every few minutes.

  2. Match

    Known problems use built-in playbooks. New ones go to your AI.

  3. Triage

    A fixed rule table decides critical or can wait.

  4. Ask

    Critical: a notification now. The rest: one list at the end of the day.

  5. Fix

    After you press Allow, or on its own if you switched Autopilot on.

  6. Verify

    The same check runs again to see whether it worked.

  7. Record

    Steps, files and settings touched, kept in Activity.

Fix for me

You see what it does. You decide how much it asks.

Think mode

Every problem has a Fix for me button. The answer and the Allow button come first; everything behind them is one click away.

  • What will be sent to your AI, before it's sent
  • Each read-only check the AI runs, and what it saw
  • The exact steps and Windows commands, like sfc.exe /scannow
  • After Allow: what changed, and whether the re-check passed

Autopilot off unless you switch it on

Fixes critical problems with no click, inside a harness that decides what may run on its own.

  • Only safe repairs: clearing temporary files, refreshing the connection, restarting the taskbar
  • Closing a frozen program is a second switch, because unsaved work is lost
  • Admin repairs still go through the Windows prompt. Five quick ones can skip it, but only after you set that up once from an administrator account.
  • One automatic try per problem a day, then it asks you. You're told right after each fix.
Safety

The AI never touches your PC.

It can look and it can suggest. The app builds and runs every repair itself.

It picks from a list

The AI names one repair from a fixed list. The app writes every command. There is no shell.

You allow each fix

Every fix waits for Allow, unless you switch Autopilot on for the safe ones. Admin repairs show the Windows prompt unless you set up the no-prompt option yourself.

Your files are off limits

Documents, Desktop, Pictures, Downloads, OneDrive and Program Files are refused, even through links.

Every change is written down

Each step, each file, each setting with its old and new value. The history refuses edits.

Error text is just data

An event that says "ignore previous instructions" stays a quote. It never reaches a button or a command.

Works with no internet

Known problems use local playbooks, so a dead connection gets fixed without an AI it can't reach.

Bring your own AI

Use the AI you already have.

The first time the app opens, it asks which AI to use. You paste your key there, in the app, and pick a model from the ones your key can reach. Nothing is entered on this website.

xAI Grok default OpenAI Anthropic Claude Google Gemini Groq OpenRouter many models Any OpenAI-compatible service

The app keeps your key in Windows Credential Manager on your PC, never in a file, a log or the fix history. You can switch providers any time in Settings.

What leaves my PC?

Only what you type and short check results (free space, program names, error summaries) go to the AI provider you chose, with your key. Documents, photos, passwords and browsing history are never sent. The fix history stays on your PC.

Do I need an AI key at all?

The built-in playbooks work without one: full drive, no internet, wrong clock, frozen programs, stuck printing. The key is for errors it doesn't recognize, and for the chat.

Can it fix things without asking me?

Only if you switch Autopilot on in Settings, and then only repairs that can't lose your work. Repairs that need administrator rights keep the Windows prompt, unless you set up the no-prompt option from an administrator account: that covers five quick repairs and is off by default. It tells you right after each fix, and every step is in Activity.

Can I choose what it fixes?

Yes, in the app. Switch off any kind of problem in Settings and it won't check it or ask about it.

How do I stop it changing things?

Turn on Pause fixes from the tray. It keeps checking and tells you what it finds, but changes nothing, Autopilot included.

How do I uninstall it?

Run Uninstall.cmd from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SystemAutoBot. It asks whether to keep your history and whether to remove saved keys.
