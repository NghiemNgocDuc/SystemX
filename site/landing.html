    Safety

    The AI never touches your PC.

    It can look and it can suggest. Only you can say yes.

    It picks from a list

    The AI names one repair from a fixed list. The app writes every command itself. There is no shell.

    Nothing runs without you

    Every fix waits for your click. Admin repairs show the Windows permission prompt every time.

    Your files are off limits

    Documents, Desktop, Pictures, Downloads, OneDrive and Program Files are refused, even through links.

    Every change is written down

    Each step, each file, each setting with its old and new value. The database refuses edits.

    Error text is just data

    An event that says "ignore previous instructions" stays a quote. It never reaches a button or a command.

    Works with no internet

    Known problems use local playbooks, so a dead connection gets fixed without an AI it can't reach.

    Bring your own AI

    Use the AI you already have.

    The first time the app opens, it asks which AI to use. You paste your key there, in the app, and pick a model from the ones your key can reach. Nothing is entered on this website.

    xAI Grok default OpenAI Anthropic Claude Google Gemini Groq gsk_ keys OpenRouter many models Any OpenAI-compatible service

    The app keeps your key in Windows Credential Manager on your PC, never in a file, a log or the fix history. You can switch providers any time in the app's Settings.

    What leaves my PC?

    Only what you type and short check results (free space, program names, error summaries) go to the AI provider you chose, with your key. Documents, photos, passwords and browsing history are never sent. The fix history stays on your PC.

    Do I need an AI key at all?

    The built-in playbooks work without one: full drive, no internet, wrong clock, frozen programs, stuck printing. The key is for errors it doesn't recognize, and for the chat.

    Can I choose what it fixes?

    Yes, in the app. Untick any kind of problem in Settings and it won't check it or ask about it. For anything it does find, you still decide each time: fix it now, do it yourself, or not now.

    How do I stop it changing things?

    Turn on Pause fixes from the tray. It keeps checking and tells you what it finds, but changes nothing.

    How do I uninstall it?

    Run Uninstall.cmd from %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\SystemAutoBot. It asks whether to keep your history and whether to remove saved keys.
